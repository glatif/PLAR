## 1. Demonstration of Learning vs Discussion of Learning

**Our working interpretation** (proposed by the project lead):

- **Demonstration of Learning (DoL)** is the **portfolio section** where the student writes about each of the 8 competencies.
- **Discussion of Learning (DoL)** is the **oral interview** with the two assessors.

**What the sources say:**

| Source | Portfolio section called | Interview called | Supports our interpretation? |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2-I Student Handbook, table of contents and section "Demonstration of Learning (DoL)" (pp.33–56) | "Demonstration of Learning (DoL)" | "Discussion of Learning" (pp.65–67) | Yes |
| P2-H Info Package p.3 / p.4 | "Demonstration of Learning" | "Discussion of Learning" box | Yes, but see the next row |
| P2-H Info Package p.5 "Eight Critical Competencies" | "...respond to in the **Discussion of Learning section of your portfolio**" | — | **No.** Uses the old name for the section. |
| P2-I Student Handbook p.35 FAQ | "When should I seek feedback on my **Discussion of Learning**?" (refers to the written section) | — | **No.** Old name. |
| P1-B Assessor Handbook p.13 "Portfolio Components" | "**Discussion of Learning (DoL)**: (previously Prior Learning Summary Part 2)" | "interview" | **No.** The handbook uses the old name for the section. |
| P1-A step 4 | — | "oral interview ('Discussion of Learning')" | Yes |
| P1-C worksheet, each competency block | — | "DoL Chair Question", "DoL Writer Question", "DoL Notes and Conclusions" (interview) | Yes |
| P2-A step 8; P2-B; P2-C; P2-D title; P2-J; P2-M; P2-O | "Demonstration of Learning" | — | Yes |
| P2-D "Speak To the Learning" | "For the **Discussion of Learning**, the assessors will be looking for..." (refers to the written section) | — | **No.** Old name. |
| EMAIL | "Demonstration of Learning section" | "Discussion of Learning/interview" | Yes |

**Conclusion:**

- The newer, student-facing materials (the email, the Steps documents, the Student Handbook's main structure, and the advisor resources) support our interpretation.
- The older Assessor Handbook and a few leftover passages still use "Discussion of Learning" for the written section.
- **Both terms are abbreviated "DoL"**, which is the main source of confusion.
- In the platform we will use **"Demonstration of Learning"** for the section and **"Discussion of Learning (interview)"** for the interview, and avoid the bare "DoL". This needs confirming: [Q-TERM-1](stakeholder-questions.md#q-term-1).
