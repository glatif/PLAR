# Roles, Responsibilities and Permissions

This page sets out who does what according to the sources, where the sources disagree, and what that means for role-based access control (RBAC). The RBAC matrix in §4 is a **proposal**. It cannot be finalised until the conflicts in §3 are resolved.

[← Back to index](index.md)

## 1. Roles inside PLAR

| Role | Documented responsibilities | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Student / candidate** | Takes the questionnaire and submits the AR. Pays the fee. Signs the Statement of Authenticity. Builds and submits the portfolio and takes part in the Discussion of Learning. "Responsible for documenting your learning." Can ask for extensions, accommodations and appeals. | P2-I p.6, p.10, p.64, p.68; P2-B PDF p.15 |
| **PLAR Advisor** (the sources name several advisors as a team) | Main point of contact. Checks Program Plan room. Reviews the AR and recommends. Handles payment and registration requests. Gives Moodle access. Sets due dates and reminders. Reviews sections and gives feedback. Runs the final checklist. Checks for double dipping and other PLAR potential. Verifies a reference letter. Sends the portfolio for approval. Arranges the interview. Connects the call. Receives and logs the report. Requests credit entry. Is the first contact for appeals. | P2-A steps 1–12, 15; P2-E; P1-B p.11, p.26; P2-I p.6, p.67–68 |
| **Booking coordinator** | One advisor helps book interviews (cc'd on Interview Letter 1). | P1-B p.120 |
| **PLAR Manager** | Assessor Handbook: "reviews portfolios for readiness for assessment, **assigns two assessors** for each portfolio, reviews PLAR reports prepared by assessors and consults with the PLAR Director for credit awards". Student Handbook: "supports the maintenance and growth of PLAR and the day-to-day operations". Advisor steps: reviews the portfolio and **recommends** two assessors; reviews the report; **sends results to the student**. | P1-B p.11; P2-I p.6; P2-A steps 10, 13–14 |
| **PLAR Director** | "Guides policy, makes **final decisions for all PLAR credit awards**, and communicates those results to the student and advisors." Resolves assessor disagreements. Receives integrity and counselling concerns. Is told when an assessment will run over the expected effort. Adjudicates appeals. Takes part in training new assessors. | P1-B p.11, p.19, p.24, p.26, p.41, p.46–47; P2-I p.6 |
| **Assessor**, in the role of **Chair** or **Writer** | Reviews the portfolio and rates it before the interview. Prepares questions with the co-assessor. Conducts the interview (the chair leads; the writer asks first in each competency). Reaches consensus. Writes the report (writer) or reviews it (chair). Submits it. Keeps notes for 2 years. Flags integrity issues, counselling needs and other PLAR potential. | P1-B p.11, p.19–26, p.39, p.41; P1-A; P1-E |
| **New / apprentice assessor** | Shadows interviews, does a practice assessment, has a mentor. | P1-B p.46–47 |
| **Administrative and CUPE staff** | Coordinate interviews, download and upload student files, give Moodle access, collect statistics, enter PLAR credit in Banner. "This support may be provided by Program Advisors, OL Administrative Assistants, etc." | P1-B p.11 |

## 2. Roles outside PLAR (that the workflow touches)

| Role | Interaction | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Program Advisors** | Create and update Program Plans. Copy PLAR on emails to new students. Confirm PLAR credit room. Send the updated Program Plan after an award. | P1-B p.7, p.35; P2-A step 1; P2-I p.70, p.72 |
| **Records Department** | Registers the student for PLAR. Enters awarded credits. | P2-A steps 4, 15; P2-I p.70 |
| **Accessibility Services / Faculty of Student Development** | Arranges accommodations and alternative assessment methods. | P1-B p.41; P2-I p.8, p.69 |
| **TRU Counselling** | Backup contact for a student in distress. | P1-B p.41 |
| **IT Service Desk** | Moodle and password support. | P2-B PDF p.15 |
| **Senior leadership ("Gord and Liesel")** | Receive the stakeholders' report. | EMAIL |

## 3. Conflicts

These need stakeholder answers before RBAC can be designed.

| Action | Sources saying **Manager** | Sources saying **Director** | Other | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Approve the portfolio for assessment** | P2-A step 10 ("sends portfolio to PLAR Manager for review"); P2-B PDF p.2 ("ask the PLAR Manager to review your portfolio to gain her approval"); P1-B p.11; P2-I p.62 ("approved by the PLAR Manager for assessment") | P2-I p.10 flowchart ("PLAR Director reviews portfolio for assessment approval"), p.64 ("the PLAR Director has approved your portfolio"); P1-B p.120 Interview Letter 1 ("the PLAR Director has reviewed your portfolio") | — | [Q-ROLE-1](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-1) |
| **Select / assign the two assessors** | P1-B p.11 ("assigns two assessors"); P2-A step 10 ("Manager **recommends** two assessors"; who confirms?) | P1-B p.19 ("based on the **advisors' and director's** evaluation") | Advisors are involved (P1-B p.19); a booking coordinator books them (P1-B p.120) | [Q-ROLE-2](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-2) |
| **Review the assessment report** | P1-A step 7; P2-A step 13; P1-F ("PLAR Manager who then reviewed and approved this report") | P1-B p.26 ("submitted to the director who reviews"); P1-E ("send this report to the PLAR Director, who will review") | An advisor reviews and logs it first (P1-B p.26) | [Q-ROLE-3](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-3) |
| **Make the final credit decision** | P1-B p.11 (the Manager "consults with the Director for credit awards") | P1-B p.11; P2-I p.6, p.67 | — | [Q-ROLE-3](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-3) |
| **Send the results to the student** | P2-A step 14 | P1-B p.11, p.26; P2-I p.10, p.67 | — | [Q-ROLE-3](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-3) |
| **Decide chair and writer roles** | — | — | P1-A step 3: the assessors decide at the pre-meeting. P1-B p.19: one "will be designated", by whom is not stated. | [Q-ROLE-4](stakeholder-questions.md#q-role-4) |

**A possible explanation (assumption).** The Assessor Handbook (c. 2020–2022) may come from before a Manager role took over some of the Director's operational duties. The newer advisor-side documents (P2-A, P2-B) consistently name the Manager. Even so, the current Student Handbook still says "Director" in several places.

## 4. RBAC implications (proposal, not a requirement)

| Capability | Proposed holder (pending §3) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Submit questionnaire / AR / portfolio sections; see own feedback and status | Student | P2-I |
| See own report and result | Student (after release) | P2-I p.67 |
| Manage the student caseload: due dates, reminders, extensions, feedback, checklist | PLAR Advisor | P2-A; P2-I p.64 |
| Verify reference letters; flag double dipping or other PLAR potential | PLAR Advisor | P2-E |
| **Approve for assessment** | Manager *or* Director (configurable) | §3 |
| **Assign assessors** | Manager *or* Director, with advisor input (configurable) | §3 |
| Schedule interviews | Advisor / booking coordinator | P1-B p.120; P2-A step 11 |
| View assigned portfolio and Program Plan | Assigned assessors only | P1-B p.19; P2-I p.14 |
| Enter **own** preliminary ratings and notes; see the co-assessor's only after both have submitted | Assessor | Independence: P1-B p.22–23 |
| Record consensus ratings and award; draft and review the report | Assessor pair (writer drafts, chair reviews) | P1-B p.24–26 |
| Review and endorse the report; send it back for clarification | Manager and/or Director | §3 |
| **Final credit decision and release** | Director (possibly delegated to the Manager) | §3 |
| Request credit entry; export to Records | Advisor | P2-A step 15 |
| Handle appeals; access assessor notes | Director | P1-B p.26 |
| Edit content: question bank, standard responses, templates, rubric | To be decided (living documents: P1-D, P2-D) | Q-FB-6 |
| Admin: users, statistics | Admin / CUPE staff and/or Manager | P1-B p.11 |
| Read-only access for Program Advisors or Records? | To be decided | Q-ROLE-5 |

Other access needs stated in the sources:

- **Confidentiality:** "Information shared in the portfolio is completely confidential" [P1-B p.44].
- Assessors may be asked to share their assessment notes [P1-B p.21].
- Assessors are told about accommodations [P1-B p.41].
- Retention is two years [P1-B p.26].
