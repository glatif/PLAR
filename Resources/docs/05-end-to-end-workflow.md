# End-to-End Workflow (Current State)

The full student journey, from first contact to credit entry and after. The detailed assessor activities are in [06-assessment-workflow.md](06-assessment-workflow.md), and the advising and feedback loop is in [07-advising-and-feedback.md](07-advising-and-feedback.md).

**Status key**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **D** | Documented: stated in a source |

## Stage 6: Assessment, Discussion of Learning, and report

Covered step by step in [06-assessment-workflow.md](06-assessment-workflow.md). In summary:

1. Both assessors pre-assess independently.
2. Pre-interview meeting.
3. Interview: the advisor connects the call and leaves.
4. Post-interview consensus meeting.
5. The writer drafts the report and the chair reviews it.
6. The report is submitted.

## Checking the project lead's understanding

> "The department receives a student's resume and subsequently handles payment, registration, record management, and Moodle access through email before the assessment process begins."

**Mostly correct, with three refinements:**

1. The **readiness questionnaire and the Program Plan check come before the resume** (steps 1.2–1.4).
2. Email is documented for: the AR submission (1.5), registration ("email to Records", 2.2), the Moodle access notice (2.3–2.4) and payment confirmation (2.1). **How payment is taken and how Moodle access is granted are not documented.**
3. "Record management" in the sources is (a) registration with Records at the start (2.2) and (b) **credit entry by Records / in Banner at the end** (7.5). No document says that a PLAR database or case file is kept, beyond assessors' notes and the report as the permanent record [P1-B p.25].
