PLAR Design System

PLAR Platform · Design System

Warm, clear and quietly confident.

One visual language for the platform, its reports and its emails. It follows Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for clarity and depth. It uses Geist for the interface, Lora for reading and headlines, and a tangerine accent that feels encouraging rather than loud.

01 · Foundations

Principles

Seven ideas guide every decision. When two options look equally good, choose the one that fits these best.

1

Content first

Portfolios, evidence and reports are the content. The interface frames them quietly, and controls float above them.

2

Clear hierarchy

Size, weight and position show what matters most. Each view has one primary action.

3

Calm by design

Students can feel exposed during PLAR. No alarming colours, countdowns or judgemental words. An Inconclusive rating is grey, never red.

4

Depth with restraint

Glass is only for navigation and controls. Content always sits on solid surfaces.

5

AI is always labelled

AI suggestions carry a marker and stay in draft style until a person accepts them. People make every decision.

6

Accessible by default

WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum. Every colour pair on this page has been checked, and targets are at least 44px.

7

One system everywhere

The same tokens drive the app, PDFs, reports, proposals and emails, so they always look like one product.

02 · Foundations

Colour

A warm, neutral canvas with one accent. Tangerine marks action and progress, and it should cover only a small part of any screen. Select a swatch to copy its value.

Tangerine scale

500 is the brand tangerine for illustration, charts and gradients. 600 is the accent fill for buttons, switches and selection, with white text at 4.57:1. 700 is for accent text and links on any light surface. Never set text in 500 or lighter on white.

Neutrals and roles

Warm greys pair with tangerine better than cool ones. Use role tokens in code, never raw hex values.

Status and AI

Use status colours sparingly, and always with an icon and a label, never colour alone. Warning is amber-brown so it doesn't look like the accent. AI uses its own indigo so a suggestion can never be mistaken for a person's work.

Contrast pairs

Measured ratios for the pairs used most. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI shapes.

Data visualisation

Sequential charts use the tangerine ramp. Categorical charts use up to six hues in this order. Always add direct labels or patterns.

07 · Foundations

Iconography

Two styles from one family, drawn on the same 24px grid so they swap without shifting. Outline is the default voice: light and calm. Filled marks something as chosen, on, or worth a glance. Icons support labels. They don't replace them, except for familiar controls that have an accessible name.

Outline and filled pairs

Outline uses a 1.75px stroke with rounded ends, matching Geist's weight. Filled versions cut their details out of a solid shape, so they read clearly at 14–16px and on coloured tiles. Action icons such as upload, download, search and close stay outline only.

When to use each

Use filled icons sparingly. They work because most icons on the screen are outline.

SituationStyleExample

Navigation, selected: sidebar item and mobile tab bar. The icon fills while the label goes semibold.Filled

24 · Guidance

Voice and content

Plain Canadian English. Encouraging, neutral and specific. Write the way a good advisor speaks.

Do
  • Add one sentence on what you'd do differently.
  • Name the action on buttons: Book interview, Release result
  • Spell out Demonstration of Learning and Discussion of Learning for students
  • Use Canadian spelling: colour, centre, program
Don't
  • Your answer is weak and incomplete.
  • OK, Yes or Submit with no object
  • Acronyms such as DoL in student views
  • Hint at an award outcome before the result is released

25 · Guidance

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is the minimum standard. Check every new screen against this list before it ships.

Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1; large text and UI shapes ≥ 3:1
Never rely on colour alone. Pair it with an icon or label
Targets ≥ 44 × 44px with at least 8px between them
Visible 3px tangerine focus ring on every interactive element
Full keyboard use; Escape closes overlays; focus returns to the trigger
Respect reduced motion and reduced transparency
Text scales to 200% without loss; body never below 16px
Captions for every recorded interview; transcripts on request
Labels above fields; errors linked with aria-describedby
Language set to en-CA; headings in order with no skipped levels
